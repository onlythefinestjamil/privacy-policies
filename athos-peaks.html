Athos Peaks — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Athos Peaks ("we", "our", the app) is a fitness and training companion. This policy explains what data the app handles and how. The short version: your health data stays yours.

1. Data the App Handles

Health & Fitness Data. With your permission, Athos Peaks reads data from Apple HealthKit (steps, heart rate, heart-rate variability, sleep, workouts, body measurements) to compute your readiness, recovery, and progress. This data is read from and written back to your HealthKit store on your device.

Body Scan & Progress Photos. The body scan feature and progress photos are processed on your device. Progress photos are faceless by design and stored locally unless you choose to sync them to your private account.

Voice Input. When you speak to the Athos assistant, your voice input is processed to answer your request and is not retained after processing.

Account Information. If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive your name and email to create and sync your account. We do not receive or store your password.

Workout & Nutrition Logs. Training programs, logged meals, and nutrition scans you create are stored with your account and on your device.

2. How Data Is Used

  • To compute your fitness metrics, recovery, and personalized training plans
  • To sync your data across your devices when signed in
  • To provide the Athos AI coaching assistant

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to anyone. We do not use your health data for advertising.

3. Storage & Security

Your data is stored on your device and, when you sign in, in your private synced account secured with industry-standard encryption. You can delete individual data or your entire account at any time from the app's settings.

4. Third-Party Services

The app integrates with: Apple HealthKit (health data, on-device), Sign in with Apple / Google Sign-In (authentication), and AI processing services used to power coaching features. Each is governed by its own privacy policy. No third party receives your data except to perform these functions.

5. Permissions

Athos Peaks requests permissions only when you use a feature that needs it (HealthKit access, camera for scans and food logging, microphone for voice). You can revoke any permission in your device Settings at any time.

6. Children

Athos Peaks is intended for users 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

7. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time. Contact us below and we will respond within 30 days.

8. Changes

We may update this policy; the latest version is always available at this address.
